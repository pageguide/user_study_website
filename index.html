Research study · Reviewing the work of web agents

PageGuide User Study

You will review a series of tasks completed by a web agent. For each task, you will see what the agent did and the answer it gave. Then you will decide whether the agent got it right, and where it went wrong if there was a problem.

  • 8 tasks
  • ~16 minutes
  • No setup or install

A 2-minute walkthrough comes first, on two practice tasks that are not part of the study. You can skip it.

What you will do

  1. 1
    Read the task and the agent's answer. Page on the left, answer on the right.
  2. 2
    Decide whether it is correct. Some tasks show the evidence the agent used; others do not.
  3. 3
    Say where it went wrong. Mark the step or claim that caused the problem.
No trick questions. This study is not testing you
We are studying how easy or hard it is to check an agent's work. Please answer from what you can actually see on the page. If you cannot tell what happened, choose that option: it is a real and useful answer.
Before you start. Time, what is recorded, anonymity
Time
About 16 minutes in one sitting. Tasks are timed to measure difficulty, not to rush you.
What is recorded
Your answers and each task's duration. No account, no email, nothing from other tabs.
Anonymity
Anonymous by default. A participant ID is optional, and only matches your run to a session.
You will need
A desktop or laptop browser, because the page and the answer sit side by side.
