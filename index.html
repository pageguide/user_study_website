🎓 PageGuide User Study

You will review what a web agent did on a series of tasks. For each one you will see the steps it took and the answer it gave, then be asked whether it got the task right — and if not, where it went wrong.

There are no trick questions and nothing is being tested about you. We are measuring how easy or hard it is to check an agent's work, so "I could not tell" is a real finding — please answer from what you can actually see rather than what you can guess.
